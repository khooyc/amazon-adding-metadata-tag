# Amazon - Adding Metadata Tag 1.6.3

## What changed

- **Detect people** now works from **Already tagged** as well as **Needs review**.
- Starting detection from **Already tagged** analyzes unique tagged images and stays on that screen when it finishes.
- A compact **with people detected** count appears beside **files shown** and **unique contents** after tagged images have detection results.
- The count follows the current Seller SKU and search filters, and tagged cards show the existing face/body badges.
- The **People detected** queue remains dedicated to untagged review work; no additional tagged-people page was added.

Detection remains a local advisory tool. It recognizes likely faces or body poses but cannot determine whether a person is AI-generated or whether a tag is required.

## Install

On Windows, download and open **Amazon-Adding-Metadata-Tag-Windows-Setup.exe** below. On macOS, choose the `arm64` DMG for Apple Silicon (M1 or newer) or the `x64` DMG for Intel.

The free installers are not code-signed. Windows SmartScreen may require **More info → Run anyway**, and macOS may require Control-clicking the app and choosing **Open** on first launch. Confirm that the download came from this repository and use `SHA256SUMS.txt` to verify it.
