Privacy & limitations

Processing stays in your browser

  • Images and videos are not uploaded by this website.
  • Face and body models are served with the site and run locally on images only.
  • JPEG, PNG, MP4, MOV, and M4V metadata is supported. Videos must be watched in full before selection.
  • Person detection is a recommendation, not proof that a person is AI-generated.
  • Always inspect downloaded files before submitting them.
